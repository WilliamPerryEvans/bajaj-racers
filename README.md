# Bajaj Racers

A driving game in the browser, set in Kivukoni, Dar es Salaam. Drive a bajaj through a city built from the real
map: roam the waterfront, pick up fares against the clock, or race five rivals. The landmarks (State House, the
Askari Monument, St. Joseph's Cathedral, Azania Front church, the Old Boma, the National Museum, the port tower and
the Kigamboni ferry) are modelled from photographs.

**Play: https://williamperryevans.github.io/bajaj-racers/**

Needs a keyboard (or a game controller) and a reasonably recent computer. Phones and tablets are not supported yet.

Keys: W A S D or arrows to drive, Space handbrake, Shift nitro, H horn, R back onto the road, C camera,
N radio, T skip an hour, P pause, M mute, Esc garage.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL).
The music on Redio Kivukoni is original, composed and synthesised in the game.
