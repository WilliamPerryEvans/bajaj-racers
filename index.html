Kivukoni · Dar es Salaam

Karibu! Welcome to Kivukoni. Drive with WASD or the arrows, Shift for nitro, Space to slide round corners. Slide a thumb on the left to steer; GAS, BRAKE, NITRO and DRIFT are on the right. Type your name for the number plate, pick a bajaj and a mode, then press DRIVE. Landmarks are on the map; to play with friends, one of you creates a room and shares the code.
Play with friends or Up to 7 players, each on their own computer or phone.

WASD or arrows to drive · Space handbrake · Shift nitro · H horn · R back onto the road · C camera · T skip an hour · P pause · N radio · M mute · Esc garage. A game controller works too.
